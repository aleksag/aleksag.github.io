ZOMBIES AREN'T REAL

A quick-spreading infection is about to break out in a real-world city. From a bird's-eye view, spend your limited budget on soldiers, turrets, mortars and air strikes. Contain the horde and save as many civilians as you can.

Pick a city to defend:
🟢 Zombie — infects civilians
🔵 Civilian — save them!
🎯 Sniper — long range, single shot
🔫 Turret — fast, short range
💥 Mortar — splash (hurts civvies!)
🛡️ Garrison — strong, multi-target
🧱 Barricade — blocks the horde
🏳️ Safe Zone — rescues civilians

Tip: place units during the calm, press Start Wave when ready. Zombies that touch a civilian turn them. Earn cash per kill. Clear all waves to win — score is civilians saved.
